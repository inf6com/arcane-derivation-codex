FAQ

  • Q. How do I back up my data?
    A. Export your YAML from the app and save it to any storage service.
  • Q. ChatGPT integration doesn’t work.
    A. Make sure your API key is correctly set in the settings screen and that your network connection is available.
  • Q. My nodes aren’t being saved.
    A. Ensure the save process completes before leaving the screen with the “Back” button.
